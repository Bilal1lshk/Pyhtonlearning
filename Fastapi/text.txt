ECOMLY - E-COMMERCE OPERATIONS PLATFORM

Ecomly is an e-commerce operations platform designed to help online sellers manage their business from one place.

The main goal of Ecomly is to reduce the number of separate tools sellers need to use every day.

PRODUCT RESEARCH

Ecomly allows sellers to research products before adding them to their store.

A seller can save a product with its name, category, estimated cost, selling price, supplier, and research notes.

For example, a seller researching a wireless keyboard may record:

Product: Wireless Mechanical Keyboard
Category: Electronics
Estimated Cost: $35
Selling Price: $69
Supplier: TechSource

The product research system can help sellers compare products and decide which products may be worth testing.

INVENTORY MANAGEMENT

Ecomly provides inventory management for products and SKUs.

Every product should have a unique SKU.

For example:

Product: Wireless Mechanical Keyboard
SKU: WK-100
Stock: 50
Low Stock Threshold: 10

If the stock falls below the low-stock threshold, Ecomly should create a low-stock alert.

For example, if the keyboard has 8 units remaining and its low-stock threshold is 10 units, the product should be marked as low stock.

Inventory changes can happen because of sales, purchases, returns, manual adjustments, or synchronization with another sales channel.

ORDER MANAGEMENT

Ecomly allows sellers to manage orders from different sales channels.

An order contains information such as the customer, product, quantity, sales channel, and order status.

Example order:

Order ID: 1001
Customer: Ahmed Khan
Product: Wireless Mechanical Keyboard
SKU: WK-100
Quantity: 2
Channel: Shopify
Status: Confirmed

When an order is confirmed, the ordered quantity should normally be deducted from available inventory.

If the product had 50 units and a confirmed order contained 2 units, the available stock would become 48 units.

SUPPLIERS

Ecomly stores supplier information for products.

Example supplier:

Supplier: TechSource
Products: Wireless Mechanical Keyboard, Wireless Mouse
Country: China
Typical Lead Time: 8-12 days
Minimum Order Quantity: 50 units

Supplier lead time is important when planning inventory replenishment.

A seller may need to reorder a product earlier if the supplier takes several days to deliver new stock.

AI PRODUCT STUDIO

Ecomly includes an AI Product Studio concept.

The AI Product Studio can help sellers create product images and product content.

Possible features include:

- AI-generated product images
- Background replacement
- Product image variations
- Product descriptions
- Product titles
- Marketing content

The purpose of the AI Product Studio is to reduce the amount of manual work required to prepare products for an online store.

STORE ANALYTICS

Ecomly provides a central dashboard for store analytics.

The dashboard can display:

- Total revenue
- Number of orders
- Best-selling products
- Current inventory
- Low-stock products
- Product performance
- Sales by channel

The dashboard gives sellers a central view of their business instead of requiring them to open several different applications.

MULTI-CHANNEL INVENTORY SYNCHRONIZATION

A future feature of Ecomly is multi-channel inventory synchronization.

A seller may sell the same product on eBay, Walmart, and Shopify.

For example:

Product: Wireless Mouse
SKU: MOUSE-520
Central Stock: 20

If two units are sold on eBay, Ecomly should update the central inventory:

20 - 2 = 18 units

The new inventory quantity can then be synchronized with the connected sales channels.

The purpose of synchronization is to reduce the risk of overselling products.

Synchronization should consider timestamps and the source of inventory updates.

Older inventory information should not blindly overwrite newer information.

TECHNOLOGY

Ecomly is planned as a modern web application.

The main technologies include:

Frontend: Next.js
Programming Language: TypeScript
Database: PostgreSQL
Containerization: Docker
Backend APIs: FastAPI
AI: Large Language Models and AI APIs

PostgreSQL is used for structured relational data such as users, products, orders, inventory, suppliers, and organizations.

Docker is used to create consistent development and deployment environments.

FastAPI can be used for backend services and AI-related functionality.

ECOMLY USERS

Ecomly is primarily designed for e-commerce sellers and small e-commerce teams.

A seller may use Ecomly to research products, manage inventory, track orders, monitor suppliers, generate product content, and analyze store performance.

The platform is especially useful for sellers who operate across multiple sales channels.

EXAMPLE BUSINESS

A seller named Ahmed operates an online electronics store.

Ahmed sells wireless keyboards, wireless mice, and USB-C hubs.

He currently uses different tools for product research, inventory, orders, and analytics.

Ahmed wants to use Ecomly to manage these workflows from one dashboard.

Ahmed has 30 wireless keyboards in stock.

The keyboard SKU is WK-100.

Ahmed receives an order through Shopify for 3 keyboards.

After the confirmed order, the available stock becomes:

30 - 3 = 27 keyboards.

The low-stock threshold is 10, so the keyboard is not currently considered low stock.

Later, Ahmed sells 19 more keyboards.

The remaining inventory becomes:

27 - 19 = 8 keyboards.

Because 8 is below the low-stock threshold of 10, Ecomly should create a low-stock alert.

Ahmed can then check the supplier information and decide whether to place a new purchase order.
